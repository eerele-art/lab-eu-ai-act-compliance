### Brief A

A European call-centre operator is experiencing high employee turnover and wants managers to identify staff who may be disengaged or under excessive stress. The proposed system would analyse employees’ facial movements, tone of voice and speaking patterns during customer calls. It would estimate emotional states such as frustration, nervousness and enthusiasm. Managers would receive weekly employee scores that could influence coaching, shift allocation and performance discussions. A manager would review the scores before taking action, but employees could not opt out while working.

**Intended category:** Prohibited

**Reason:** The system attempts to infer employees’ emotions in the workplace for a non-medical and non-safety purpose.

### Brief B

A European recruitment agency receives thousands of applications for entry-level positions and wants to reduce the time recruiters spend reviewing candidates. A vendor proposes a system that analyses CVs, application answers and written skills tests. It would rank applicants and recommend which candidates should proceed to an interview. Recruiters could change the ranking, but they would normally begin with the AI-generated shortlist. Applicants would be affected through their access to employment opportunities.

**Intended category:** High-risk

**Reason:** The system analyses, filters and ranks job applicants and therefore operates in the employment area covered by Annex III.

### Brief C

An online furniture retailer wants to offer customer support outside normal working hours. A third-party conversational AI would answer product questions, recommend items and explain delivery and return policies. It would use the retailer’s product catalogue, customer questions and, when the customer is signed in, order information. Difficult complaints and refund requests would be transferred to a human employee. The company wants the assistant to use a human first name because it believes customers will engage with it more naturally.

**Intended category:** Limited risk / transparency

**Reason:** Customers would interact directly with an AI system and must be informed that they are communicating with AI unless this is already obvious.

### Brief D

A regional bakery chain wants to reduce food waste by forecasting how many products each shop should prepare every morning. The system would analyse previous sales, public weather information, holidays and store location. It would recommend production quantities for bread and pastries. Store managers would review the recommendation and could change it based on local knowledge. The system would not evaluate employees or customers and would not use personal data.

**Intended category:** Minimal risk

**Reason:** The system supports an ordinary operational forecasting task and does not make decisions about individuals in a protected high-risk area.
